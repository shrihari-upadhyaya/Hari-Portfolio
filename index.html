About Me

Product manager with an engineer's toolkit.

I've spent over six years in industry across EdTech and BFSI, and the last three building products. I enjoy the full arc of the job: talking to users, writing the PRD, working through build trade-offs with engineering, and then watching the numbers after launch.

My route into product was not the usual one. After three years as an RPA engineer at TCS, I stepped away to prepare full-time for the UPSC Civil Services Examination. Two years of breaking down vast, ambiguous syllabi into daily decisions taught me how to stay structured under pressure, and I returned to industry with exactly that discipline. It is how I still approach every product problem.

At Kenverse, I own the interview module on Salesforce CRM for accounts like Manipal Institutes and SP Jain. By iterating on the module with SQL analysis, Power BI dashboards, and A/B tests, I cut process turnaround by 20%. Earlier, at Digii, I led implementations for NLSIU, IIT Madras, NALSAR, JGI, and VIT, delivering every project on time and lifting platform adoption by 25%.

What helps me most is range. I can write the PRD, query the data to check my own assumptions, and speak comfortably with engineers about how something gets built. I am now extending that range into AI through LLMs, RAG, and prompt engineering, alongside an Executive MBA at IIM Kozhikode.

  • SQL, Power BI and A/B testing
  • LLMs, RAG and prompt engineering
