About Me

Products are systems. I build them end-to-end.

Products aren't just features — they're systems that change how people work. That belief has driven my 3+ years in product — building on 6+ years in industry across EdTech and BFSI — where I've owned products end-to-end: from discovery, PRDs, and roadmapping through launch and iteration.

My path into product wasn't linear — and that's my strength. I spent two years preparing intensively for the UPSC Civil Services Examination (2022–2023), one of India's toughest competitive exams. It built the mental discipline I rely on every day: breaking ambiguous, high-stakes problems into structured decisions, synthesising large volumes of information under pressure, and thinking in systems, not silos. When I returned to industry, I brought that rigour straight into product work.

At Kenverse, I own the interview module on Salesforce CRM for strategic accounts like Manipal Institutes and SP Jain — cutting process turnaround by 20% and driving adoption through data-informed iteration in SQL, Power BI, and A/B testing. Before that, at Digii (formerly CollPoll), I led full-cycle implementations for NLSIU, IIT Madras, NALSAR, JGI, VIT, and more — 100% on-time delivery and a 25% lift in platform adoption. My foundation is technical: at TCS, I built Blue Prism automation bots that cut cheque verification time by 80% and report generation from 3 hours to 30 minutes.

What sets me apart is range: I can write the PRD, query the data to validate it, and speak fluently with engineering about how it's built. That range now extends to AI — building with LLMs, RAG, and prompt engineering — as I pursue an Executive MBA at IIM Kozhikode.
